Privacy Policy - Wizard/Play 9/QQ Scorekeeper

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Wizard/Play 9/QQ Scorekeeper app ("the App"), developed by Aaron Buffett, handles your information.

Wizard/Play 9/QQ Scorekeeper is part of the Game Night app series.

Information We Collect

The App is designed to operate locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers unless explicitly enabled by the user through a specific feature (such as sharing or export functionality).

Local Data Storage

All data such as player profiles, game history, and settings are stored locally on your device using on-device storage.

Data Sharing

The App does not share your personal data with third parties.

The App does not use analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party advertising services.

In-App Purchases

If applicable, purchases are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Third-Party Services

The App may use platform-provided services (such as Apple or Google) for distribution and purchases. These services may collect data as governed by their own privacy policies.

Data Security

All data is stored locally on your device using platform-provided storage mechanisms.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by removing the app from your device or using any in-app data reset options if available.

Camera Usage

The app may request access to your device camera solely for scanning QR codes. No images or video are recorded, stored, or transmitted. Camera access is only used in real time for this feature.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy
