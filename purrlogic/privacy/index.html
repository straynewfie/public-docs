Privacy Policy - Purrlogic

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Purrlogic ("the App"), developed by Aaron Buffett, handles information.

Information We Collect

Purrlogic stores gameplay information such as progress, scores, times, achievements, settings, and game history needed to provide the App's features.

Local Data Storage

Gameplay progress and related information may be stored locally on your device so that your progress and settings can be preserved between sessions.

Online Game Services

Purrlogic may communicate with online services to provide features such as daily challenges, official results, rankings, leaderboards, and other game functionality. Information used for these features is limited to what is needed to operate the game and its online features.

Data Sharing

Purrlogic does not sell personal information. The App does not use third-party advertising services or advertising tracking.

Third-Party Services

The App may use service providers and platform services to operate game functionality and distribute the App. These services may process limited technical or game-related information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy practices.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by the App and its online services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored App data can be removed by deleting the App or by using any in-app reset options that are available. If you have questions or requests concerning information associated with Purrlogic's online services, contact us using the address below.

Children's Privacy

Purrlogic does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page.

Contact
