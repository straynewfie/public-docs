Purrlogic Help

Rules, controls, scoring, and navigation for Purrlogic.

How to Play

Find one hidden object in every region to clear the board.

Rules

  • Exactly 1 hidden object per region.
  • Exactly 1 hidden object per row.
  • Exactly 1 hidden object per column.
  • Hidden objects cannot touch, including diagonally.

Controls

  • Tap a cell to toggle an X (elimination).
  • Drag to paint or erase Xs across cells.
  • Double-tap to commit a placement.

Action Bar

  • CLEAR Xs removes all rule-out X markers from the board.
  • PAUSE pauses gameplay and the timer until you resume.
  • EXPOSE uncovers one correct answer when you're stuck. The exposed answer earns no Found or Reveal credit, ends your current streak, prevents a Perfect Game, and adds 60 seconds to your time. Health is unaffected.

Using EXPOSE also reduces the Found maximum. Example: FOUND 0/8 becomes FOUND 0/7 after one EXPOSE.

Tips

  • X marks are eliminations. They are not mistakes and do not cost Health.
  • You have 3 Health. A wrong commit removes one Health.
  • If Health reaches zero, the attempt fails and you can try again.
  • Adding or removing an X breaks your streak for scoring.

How to Score

Final Score is the sum of Cleared, Health Bonus, Reveal Bonus, Streak Bonus, and Time Score.

Cleared

Each correct placement is worth 1,000 points. For a finished puzzle, Cleared equals grid size × 1,000.

Example: 8×8 → Cleared 8,000.

Health Bonus

On a successful clear, remaining Health awards 1,000 points each, up to 3 Health. Wrong commits do not subtract score; they only reduce Health.

  • 3 Health → 3,000.
  • 2 Health → 2,000.
  • 1 Health → 1,000.
  • 0 Health ends the attempt before a clear.

Reveal Bonus

A reveal qualifies when its order matches its row number. The 1st correct reveal in Row 1 qualifies, the 2nd in Row 2 qualifies, and so on. Each qualifying reveal awards +250.

Example: the first reveal in Row 3 does not qualify. If the second reveal is in Row 2, that second reveal does qualify.

Streak Bonus

Streak grows with consecutive correct reveals. A wrong commit, or adding/removing an X, breaks the streak. Streak points begin on the second consecutive correct reveal and increase by 100 points at each awarding step.

Perfect Game

Perfect Game requires full Health, full Reveal, and full Streak on the successful clear. Time Score does not affect Perfect Game.

Time Score

Time Score awards +1 point for each whole second under 10:00 of active play time, plus one qualifying completion-time cutoff bonus. Cutoffs do not stack, and Time Score never goes below 0.

  • Under 30 seconds: +750 cutoff bonus.
  • 30–59 seconds: +500.
  • 60–89 seconds: +300.
  • 90–119 seconds: +200.
  • 120–149 seconds: +100.
  • 150–179 seconds: +50.
  • 3:00 or slower: +0 cutoff bonus.

Daily Story

Daily Story uses the same per-chapter scoring, then adds a Story Lives Bonus after all five chapters. Failed Story attempts bank 0 for that chapter.

How to Navigate

Home is the main hub for Purrlogic.

Home Sections

  • The Climb contains progressive Levels and Mastery.
  • The Dailies contains shared Single and Story challenges.
  • The Sandbox contains Practice, Tutorials, and Achievements.

NEW Indicators

  • Purple NEW chrome marks something newly unlocked or newly earned that you have not reviewed yet.
  • Follow NEW into the destination, then into any highlighted category or item.
  • NEW clears after you view or acknowledge the item under the normal rules for that feature.

Settings

Tap the gear to open Settings. Music, Effects, Haptics, Data, and Help are available there. Help opens this documentation and is separate from the interactive Tutorials in the game.
