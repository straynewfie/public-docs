Buffett Apps — Authentication Privacy Policy

This policy describes authentication information processed when participating apps use Buffett Apps sign-in. Each app's separate privacy policy covers its own data and features.

Information processed

When you sign in with Google, the authentication service may receive your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information such as name or profile picture, depending on the information Google provides. Email sign-in processes your email address and sign-in verification information. Authentication services also process session tokens and limited security or diagnostic information necessary to operate sign-in.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate you, maintain account sessions, help secure access, and support account-related requests. Signing in does not automatically make you a member of any particular league or group.

Service providers

Google provides Google sign-in, and Supabase provides authentication infrastructure. These providers may process authentication information under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell authentication information.

Storage and retention

Authentication records may be stored by Supabase, and session information may be stored on your device. Information is retained while needed to operate accounts and for legitimate security or legal purposes. Exact retention can depend on the provider and the participating app.

Your choices and deletion

You can stop using sign-in and request account deletion by contacting geoffbuffett@gmail.com. Please identify the app and email associated with the account. Requests may require identity verification. Deleting an authentication account does not necessarily delete historical league results or other records held under an application's separate policy.

Security

We use authentication infrastructure and reasonable safeguards intended to protect account access, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This shared sign-in service is not specifically directed at children. Participating apps may set additional age requirements.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Questions and privacy requests: geoffbuffett@gmail.com.
