Cavern — Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your Cavern app account and associated personal information.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Cavern and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Select Delete Account and follow the confirmation instructions.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access your account, email geoffbuffett@gmail.com with the subject Cavern Account Deletion Request. Include the email address associated with your account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request. You do not need to reinstall the app to make this request.

What is deleted or retained

Deleting your Cavern account removes your app sign-in account and associated authentication information. Account-linked personal data is deleted or disassociated where applicable, subject to legal or operational retention requirements.

Cavern Cup league history is separate from app accounts. Historical player names, game results, scores, standings, and championship records may be retained to preserve the accuracy of past competitions, even after an account is deleted. To request review or correction of league records, contact the address above.

Deletion requests made by email will be reviewed and processed after any necessary identity verification. If information must be retained, we will explain the reason and applicable retention period when responding to your request.

Questions

Contact geoffbuffett@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
