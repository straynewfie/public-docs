Cavern — Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the Cavern mobile app handles information.

League records

Cavern displays historical and current Cavern Cup poker league information, including player names, season participation, standings, scores, schedules, game results, and championship records. These records may be accessible to guests without signing in.

Optional accounts

Users may choose to sign in using Google or an email link. Sign-in uses Supabase authentication. Account information may include an email address, provider identifier, basic profile information, and authentication/session information. See the Buffett Apps authentication privacy policy for additional details.

Use and storage

League information is used to display league history and standings. Supabase is used for online league data and authentication. Session information may be stored on your device to keep you signed in.

Player profile linking

An account is not automatically associated with an existing poker player record. Account-to-player verification and administrative features may be introduced later, with corresponding policy updates.

Sharing and public visibility

League names and performance records displayed in the app may be visible to other users, including guests. We do not sell personal information. Third-party providers such as Supabase and Google may process information needed to provide their services.

Retention and deletion requests

To request correction or deletion of an account or ask about league records, contact geoffbuffett@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity. Historical competition results may need to be retained to preserve league standings and championship records; requests concerning those records will be reviewed individually.

Security and children

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. Cavern is not specifically directed at children.

Updates

This policy may change as new features are introduced. The latest version will be published here.
